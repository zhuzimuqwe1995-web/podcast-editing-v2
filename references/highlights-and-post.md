# Highlights, clips, trailers, and post text cues

## Eligibility

A candidate must appear in the actual retained SRT or be explicitly restored by the reviewer. Do not recover an attractive line from the AI deletion list without confirmation.

## Explainable assessment

Use high/medium/low assessments with source evidence for standalone clarity, opening hook, specificity, contrast, emotional or practical payoff, qualification integrity, B-roll potential, platform fit, and sensitivity risk. “爆点” is editorial judgment, not a guaranteed performance forecast.

Reject or lengthen a candidate when its hook depends on deleted context, drops a qualifier, obscures speaker attribution, merges speakers, or needs invented narration.

## Allocation

Before final allocation, obtain requested clip and trailer counts unless already provided. Vary topic, emotional register, structure, and audience value. Mark continuous excerpts and reordered montage separately. A montage lists source segments in execution order and must not manufacture sequence or causation.

## B-roll and post cues

B-roll is a text suggestion tied to a retained interval and a purpose: explain, establish, cover a jump, provide pacing space, or support emotion. Record suggested subject, material type, duration, misleading associations to avoid, and rights note. Never imply that suggested footage was supplied or reviewed.

Large text distinguishes exact quotation from editor-written title. Sound cues specify trigger words, intensity, purpose, and restraint. Avoid comic or sensational effects for injury, illness, death, allegations, legal/medical claims, or serious safety material unless the user explicitly chooses that tone.
