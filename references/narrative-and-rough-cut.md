# Narrative diagnosis and conservative rough cut

## Diagnosis

Map the corrected program by function: opening promise, question, answer, example, escalation, qualification, tangent, repetition, transition, payoff, and close. For each issue cite source timecodes and an exact anchor quote, then state expected benefit and possible loss.

Do not treat natural speech as a defect by default. Distinguish style from structural delay, duplicated meaning, broken causality, and missing payoff.

## Decisions

- `keep`: required for meaning, tone, attribution, qualification, or structure.
- `delete`: a whole verified cue can be removed without changing claim, attribution, safety condition, or required context.
- `review`: cue-internal cut, overlap, uncertain word, sensitive interpretation, reordered sequence, or any disputed boundary.

Every decision needs source cue IDs, exact interval, source text, concrete reason, evidence, confidence, and reviewer state.

## Deliver together

1. complete corrected source-timeline SRT;
2. suggested source-timeline rough-cut SRT;
3. deletion comparison table;
4. retained-cue trace map;
5. narrative diagnosis and chapter map;
6. unresolved words and DaVinci review questions.

Never ripple later timecodes in the suggested SRT. Never call the suggestion the actual retained program.
