# Context review

Review only audio, SRT, source mappings, decisions, and text suggestions.

## Checks

1. The final retained SRT belongs to the recorded project version.
2. Exact quotations match the audio-verified corrected source.
3. Removed or reordered passages preserve meaning, attribution, qualification, and causality.
4. Every final segment maps to source timecodes.
5. B-roll, large-text, and sound suggestions do not create facts or misleading associations.
6. Editor-written titles are not presented as speaker quotations.
7. Platform, rights, and external editing tasks are listed for the human editor.

## Status

- `context_ready`: the supplied text artifacts are coherent and traceable.
- `needs_revision`: correctable text or mapping issues remain.
- `blocked`: missing or ambiguous evidence prevents a reliable review.

For every problem give severity, exact object or interval, source evidence, impact, and required action. State that the result does not review video, subtitle burn-in, lip sync, sound mix, or an editing project.
